---
title: "Real Estate Wealth Is Not a Monolith: Non-Primary Real Estate and Its Role in Wealth Concentration"
kind: working-paper
year: 2026
authors: with S. Filauro, I. Marx and J. C. Gornick
venue: Under review, The Journal of Economic Inequality · Stone Center Working Paper No. 127
order: 10
---
Housing is usually treated as the asset of the middle class. Using harmonised HFCS and SCF microdata, we show that second homes and other non-primary property behave very differently, and drive a large share of wealth concentration in Europe and the US.
