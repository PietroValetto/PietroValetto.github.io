I'm a PhD researcher at the [Centre for Social Policy Herman Deleeck](https://www.uantwerpen.be/en/research-groups/csb/), University of Antwerp, and a Junior Academic Fellow at the European Policy Centre in Brussels. I study how generative AI is changing European labour markets, and what that means for inequality and for how welfare states pay for themselves.

I also write [Popular Economics](/popular-economics/), a newsletter and video series that explains economic research to people who don't read economics papers.

**Currently working on:** what happened to the incomes of AI-exposed workers in Europe after ChatGPT, whether young workers are the "canaries in the coal mine", and how second homes drive wealth concentration.
