I'm a PhD researcher at the [Centre for Social Policy Herman Deleeck](https://www.uantwerpen.be/en/research-groups/csb/), University of Antwerp. I study wealth inequality and poverty trends across the European Union and the United States: in particular, how household net worth affects eligibility for welfare programmes, and how housing wealth is distributed.

I'm passionate about using the tools of economics to understand what's happening in the world around us and to help guide policymakers' decisions. That's why I also work on projects measuring the impact of AI on the EU labour market, including as a Junior Academic Fellow at the European Policy Centre in Brussels.

I believe academics need to do a better job of communicating their research. That's why I make content online and write [Popular Economics](/popular-economics/), a newsletter and video series on the economic forces behind everyday life.
